## Preview
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. All pages are static HTML/CSS/JS and share the same navigation and footer.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `Studio` and the placeholder brand mark with your company name/logo.
2. Replace `hello@example.com` in `contact.html` and `assets/script.js` with your real email.
3. Replace concept portfolio cards with real projects or keep them clearly labelled as concepts.
4. Do not publish invented client logos, results or testimonials.
5. Review privacy policy and terms for your actual business.
6. Add real contact/social URLs, domain metadata, favicon and analytics only if needed.

## Notes
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile.
- Scroll reveal animations, floating mockups and hover transitions.
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No framework, build step, npm or VS Code is required to preview the static pages.
- The contact form uses `mailto:` and does not store submissions. A production form needs a backend or form service.
